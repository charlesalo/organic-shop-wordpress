Everything you might want to know about ordering, delivery, and how we farm. Still stuck? Reach out anytime.

What does "certified organic" actually mean here?

Every product carries USDA organic certification, meaning no synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers, and no GMOs. We go further with regenerative practices like crop rotation and cover cropping to keep our soil genuinely alive.

How fresh is the produce when it arrives?

We harvest to order. Your fruits and vegetables are picked the same morning your order ships, so most items are less than 24 hours off the plant when they reach your door.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver throughout the greater Sacramento region, with weekly routes to Davis, Folsom, and Roseville. Enter your zip code at checkout to confirm coverage.

Do I return the glass milk bottles?

Yes please. Rinse and leave your empty bottles out on your next delivery day and we will collect, sanitize, and reuse them. A small deposit is added at checkout and refunded when bottles come back.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Absolutely, up until the evening before your delivery day. After that our team is already harvesting, so changes are not possible, but you can always adjust the following order.
