Since 1998

We started with two acres, a secondhand tractor, and a stubborn belief that food should taste like the soil it grew in.

Farming that gives back to the land

Everything we grow is certified organic, but the certification is just the floor. We rotate our crops, plant cover between seasons, and let our pastures rest so the soil stays alive. Healthy ground means resilient plants, and resilient plants mean produce with real flavor and real nutrition.

Our dairy comes from a small herd that grazes open pasture year round. No confinement, no growth hormones, no shortcuts. We bottle in returnable glass because good milk deserves better than plastic.

From our fields to your table

We harvest to order. When you place an order, we pick that morning and pack it the same day, so what arrives at your door was still growing hours earlier. It is the closest thing to walking the rows yourself.
